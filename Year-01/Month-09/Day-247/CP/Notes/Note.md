# Big O Notation

1. `How to represent Time Complexity?`: 

	1. `At least number of operations`: this is achieved when our `input`  is the most `favourable input` for our algorithm and this is also known as `Best time complexity` or `THe lower bound time complexity`. Also this is the `complexity` below which our algorithm can't go anyway this is the `lower bound`.

	2. `At most number of operations`: this is achieved by algorithm when our `input` is the `worse input` for our algorithm and this is also known as `Worst time complexity` or `The uper bound time complexity`.
    
	3. `Average number of operations`: `at most or at least` are achieved for some `favourable` `input` wherease `average` is achieved across all test cases , means usually our `complexity` goes through this particular point through out given `inputs`.
